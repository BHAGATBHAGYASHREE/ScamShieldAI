Product Differentiation

“Not Just Scam / Not Scam.”

A binary label doesn't protect the user. Consumers need reasoning, category context, and actionable safety directives.

01 — DETECT

Is This Suspicious?

Calculates a calibrated 0–100 danger score so consumers know the exact confidence of the threat rather than an opaque binary guess.

02 — EXPLAIN

Why Was It Flagged?

Extracts specific deception tokens: shortened URL redirects, private phone callback numbers, Shannon character entropy, and coercion language.

03 — CLASSIFY

What Scam Type Is It?

Maps threats to verified taxonomy: Payment & Refund Lures, Fake Orders, Bank KYC Blocks, Utility Disconnections, and Delivery Traps.

Feature Defense

Why These Specific Features?

Every feature represents a measurable signal that separates authentic communication from malicious manipulation.

FINANCIAL

Currency symbols (₹, Rs), amounts, and payment requests indicate financial transaction stakes.

ACTION & URGENCY

Urgent deadlines (“immediately”, “today”) coerce victims to act before critical thinking engages.

IDENTITY & ACCESS

OTP, KYC, and account verification keywords signal credential and authentication harvesting.

COMMUNICATION

Shortened redirection links and callback telephone numbers provide the scammer's extraction mechanism.

Raw Text Alone

Harder to interpret individual threat triggers; treats tokens as independent bags without domain context.

TF-IDF N-grams

Captures important deceptive bi-grams (“order placed”, “call immediately”) with zero GPU compute overhead.

Engineered Heuristics

Provides explicit, measurable threat signals that make the model transparent and explainable to users.
